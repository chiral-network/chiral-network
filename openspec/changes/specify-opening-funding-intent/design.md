## Context and scope

Authority is the read-only [book](../../../docs/chiral-book.md), especially **Approved non-reserving quotes**, **Shared signing-wallet directory**, **Agreements and exact funding order** and **Settlement and recovery risks**. The author's 2026-11-14 answer supersedes public named-wallet quote quotas. The new capability specifies the paid opening's pre-credit protocol: one original, authenticated capacity admission, durable provider acceptance, client acknowledgement-before-broadcast and original-operation recovery. It does not certify the whole financial lifecycle.

Reuse `inference-wire-foundation` for identity, money, signing and opening calldata; the pending commercial change for quote/execution terms; and owner-control for one-use authority. For quote retention/admission, use the pending [`specify-non-reserving-quotes`](../archive/2026-11-21-specify-non-reserving-quotes/design.md) replacement delta and aligned [`specify-proposal-rate-budgets`](../archive/2026-11-21-specify-proposal-rate-budgets/design.md), not superseded canonical payer-slot/rate requirements. This opening contract consumes their boundaries without redefining quote fields/lifetime, source controls, challenge authorization, prices or per-inference admission/performance rules. Safe-frontier settlement, reversal/loss accounting, explicit cancellation, fees and credential lifecycle need their own complete acceptance.

## Decisions

### One original, two protected persistence boundaries

The consumer saves selected signed terms, then `ChainClient::prepare_payment_once` owns the original in the shared protected wallet journal. Consumer saved-account state and that journal are not one atomic store: the recoverable gap is journal commit before local raw-byte/hash save. Resume reads the original journal first; a missing consumer copy can be recovered, but an existing copy disagreeing with the journal is not permission to sign again.

Retain the stable `open:<quote_id>` operation and the existing network/wallet storage scope. A provider deployment directory is not a replacement shared nonce journal. Processes with separate journal copies need separate signing wallets; this is not a multi-host SQLite coordinator. Existing terminal/reorg nonce ownership remains in force.

No new database, provider escrow contract, operation identifier, schema conversion or compatibility shim is proposed. Native estimation uses the actual eSpace RPC estimate, bounded by the structural minimum and 1,000,000 gas, rather than a guessed multiplier. Provider admission uses the full signed limit and maximum price, including typed envelopes, not merely the value or minimum intrinsic gas.

### Acknowledgement binds a hash, not a newly invented quote digest field

The existing six-field `FundingIntent` is enough: its original transaction hash binds signed bytes whose calldata commits to the complete quote. The consumer must validate those bytes against its own selected agreement, not treat an opaque hash or provider response as a replacement for that reconstruction. Preserve the existing INTENT domain and strict payload/envelope decoding.

Require registration within the original signed quote interval. Preserve the consumer's 30-second future guard as a receiver plausibility check, not a 30-second acknowledgement lifetime. Recovery after quote expiry remains valid if registration was timely; independent clock-plausibility checks still apply.

Original raw representation is deliberately strict: the provider's retained string must be replayed verbatim. Uppercase hex may decode to the same transaction during initial envelope validation, but changing the string after acceptance is a conflict, not permission to update the stored original. Native recovery already retains that original string.

### Fresh admission is not cached recovery or a chain reservation

Public quotes are signed, overbookable terms. The parent contract retains at most 10,000 standalone unaccepted quotes provider-wide and removes both the eight-pending and twelve-per-minute named-wallet gates. Those records neither reserve paid leases nor consume free-account capacity. Independently, quote signing uses 600 pre-signing work units per ledger/effective minute and committed issuance uses 600 insertions per ledger/effective minute, with separate durable non-regressing clocks. A claimed work unit remains spent after failure; failed insertion rolls back committed-issuance charges and clock movement. Acknowledgement/expiry/opening may release storage but refund neither successful issuance nor claimed work. Work initialization, adoption and source-identity rules remain owned by the parent/rate changes, not redefined here.

The production control handler authenticates and binds the payer before checking for an existing acknowledgement. Fresh admission then checks funding/readiness and compatible qualified execution, obtains current chain evidence, checks qualification again, and enters ledger registration with fresh availability observations. These checks are not a lock spanning an external engine, operator flags and the chain; do not promise commit-time engine liveness or indefinitely reserved chain balance.

Preserve the independent 10,000 active-intent ceiling. An active lease is an unopened agreement whose admission deadline is strictly later than registration time. Its deadline is the quote's original expiry, not a sliding retry timeout. After payer authentication and exact quote/payment validation, the final capacity check and acknowledgement/evidence insertion occur in the same ledger transaction. Competing writers cannot consume the same last lease. Historical evidence and permanent sender/nonce ownership do not consume that active lease after release. This neither promises inference concurrency nor replaces independent quote, challenge or per-request reservation/performance policies.

A full fresh paid admission returns 429/`rate_limit` without acknowledgement, accepted intent, account, credit or submission. Neither an honest provider nor a conforming client broadcasts that refused original. Earlier acceptance is not revocable overbooking: later full capacity, expiry or restart cannot erase its terms/nonce ownership or justify refusing valid-payment recovery/credit. Canonical successful execution and the healthy `safe` frontier remain mandatory.

Cached acknowledgement recovery occurs before new readiness, qualification and RPC gates, but after owner authorization. It returns the original signed acknowledgement without refreshing lease/time, allocating a new pending quote or requiring fresh quote-retention/work/issuance/opening-capacity allowance. Draining, expiry, a now-spent nonce and unavailable RPC do not revoke earlier acceptance. Exhausted challenge/control admission may still delay fresh authority; durable retention is not an availability guarantee.

Free first opening instead applies its existing authenticated account limits atomically (fewer than eight owned accounts and fewer than 10,000 total accounts before creation), with no fake payment or funding-intent. These are not public quote allowances and are not repeated for an accepted paid opening or existing-account recovery. Accepted-account, top-up and settlement recovery acquire no fresh quote/work/rate/opening-capacity gate; their existing authority and financial evidence requirements remain. The separate HTTP root-recovery defect below is not solved by this capacity distinction.

### Producer and consumer registration-time consistency

`BillingStore::register_funding_intent` samples its existing injected clock after acquiring the immediate transaction and now enforces `issued_at <= now < expires_at`. Before task 2.4 it checked only expiry; a regressed provider time could persist an acknowledgement that `consumer::funding_intent_matches` rejects. Refuse the fresh attempt with `invalid_quote` rather than sign an unusable timestamp. No logical quote/challenge watermark supplies or clamps signed funding time, and native validation is unchanged.

`registration_preserves_signed_time_boundaries_and_cached_recovery` exercises 99, 100, 399, 400 and 401 for genuine signed `[100,400)` terms, plus an unavailable clock. Candidate originals are genuinely signed transactions; prior paid-account history is an explicit post-verification ledger prerequisite. Refusal must preserve all snapshotted terms, nonce ownership, rates, money, credentials and request evidence across reopening, then permit the same original at issuance. Successful insertion must change only the matching standalone quote and complete retained intent; the acknowledgement's signature, fields, exact sampled time and original expiry are checked. Reopened cached acceptance cannot consult a regressed, expired or unavailable fresh clock or renew its lease, even while new funding is disabled.

`registration_samples_time_after_waiting_for_the_writer` uses two independent ledger connections and a third SQLite writer that actually holds an immediate transaction. A thread-local one-shot busy-handler/channel handshake proves the registration caller encountered that lock before advancing its injected time to 99 or 400. No sleeps, accepted-timestamp rewriting or arrival-time backdating are used. An independent zero-timeout writer probe inside the clock callback must be excluded by registration's own write transaction after the blocker releases. Both waits must refuse without any durable evidence change. Ten-second channel timeouts bound test deadlocks; they do not simulate elapsed funding time. This is deterministic ledger acceptance, not elapsed HTTP/native lost-response recovery, live-chain spendability or power-loss qualification.

### Durable disclosure and ambiguous outcomes

The provider's immediate SQLite transaction owns acknowledgement/quote/raw evidence, nonce uniqueness and lease retention; success returns only after commit. Apply the pending replacement retention handoff: delete only the matching standalone quote after retaining the complete accepted original atomically, releasing provider-wide quote storage rather than a payer slot. Signing, insertion or commit failure must not produce success or partially release retention. Archived handoff tests supply useful historical conservation evidence, not authority to preserve old wallet ceilings.

The client verifies the acknowledgement, durably saves it, durably saves broadcast-started intent, and sends the original journal entry. Broadcast-started describes possible submission, not inclusion or credit. A lost/mismatched RPC response leaves ambiguity; recovery reconciles and, when allowed, resends that original. It does not sign a fee bump or prepare another payment.

The provider receives broadcastable signed bytes before the consumer sees an acknowledgement. An honest provider must not broadcast a definitely refused original, but receipt still technically lets a dishonest provider do so. A lost registration reply/timeout is not definite refusal: acceptance may already have committed. Recover the exact original acknowledgement/transaction rather than automatically obtaining a replacement quote or payment. The existing [CLI workflow](../../../README.md#developer-api-quick-start) discloses this trust boundary and directs `pending`/`resume`. Neither client ordering nor an off-chain rejection is cryptographic escrow, proof that no transfer occurred or a refund.

### Parent 2.6: native restart recovery

Inventory for parent task 2.6, 2026-11-21. Each clause and scenario cell maps to the requirement text it tests, to passing tests whose assertions were read in source, and to the gap this step filled. Unit tests are `consumer::tests::…` in [`consumer.rs`](../../../src-tauri/src/consumer.rs) and `chain_client::tests::…` in [`chain_client_tests.rs`](../../../src-tauri/src/chain_client_tests.rs). **ACK** marks `inference_funding_acknowledgement_test`, which this step ran. **ADM** marks `inference_funding_admission_test` and **OCC** `inference_owner_consumer_test`; this step reran only the cited tests of each. `inference_owner_control_test` was neither run nor edited. All RPC is scripted and loopback-only, and the in-crate provider is a mock; nothing here is live-chain, process-kill, fsync or power-loss evidence.

Requirements cited: **R** *Restart recovery does not manufacture another opening*; **P** *Native preparation durably owns one exact opening transaction*; **N** *Native broadcast follows verified durable acknowledgement*.

Every cell must show one original operation with its exact signed bytes retained; no replacement signature, meaning no second journal operation or nonce; and no submission the requirements do not permit. Evidence for "no replacement signature" is one of: an unchanged journal (no row inserted), a single journal operation at nonce 0 with the original raw and hash after recovery, or the absence of the fee, gas-estimate and balance reads that must precede any fresh signature (`fresh_signing_reads`, added by this step). Evidence for "no unpermitted submission" is the recorded `eth_sendRawTransaction` calls, each of which the in-crate `funding_rpc` handler accepts only when the saved file already holds the verified acknowledgement, the start marker and the same raw (task 2.5, statement 6).

**1. Missing or inconsistent journal refuses replacement.**

- *Spec.* R: "If consumer state already contains original signed bytes but the journal is missing or contains different bytes, recovery SHALL stop without signing or submitting a replacement." Scenario *Missing or inconsistent journal refuses replacement*.
- *Existing.* ACK `native_recovery_refuses_missing_or_inconsistent_original_journal` (task 2.6.1). With a genuinely signed saved original and accepted acknowledgement, it deletes the journal row, or replaces it with a correctly signed different original for the same quote at nonce 1. Reopened `resume` and reopened repeated `fund` each fail with no RPC attempt and no provider exchange; the saved file is byte-identical and the journal rows equal the damaged rows, so nothing was inserted or re-signed; the provider keeps its acknowledgement and quote with no account, funding or fee row. Restoring the exact original row reaches RPC with the unchanged saved file, no new registration and no submission. `missing_journal_never_replaces_a_saved_original_transaction` is narrower (placeholder bytes): `refusing to sign a replacement`, no submission, no provider event and an empty journal.
- *Gap.* None.

**2. Journal committed before the local copy.**

- *Spec.* R: "If the journal committed before the consumer saved its copy, recovery SHALL retrieve that exact original and repair the missing local copy, not allocate another nonce." Scenario *Crash checkpoints keep one original*, "journal commit".
- *Existing.* `funding_crash_boundaries_resume_original_bytes_and_accepted_expired_terms` checkpoint 0: the journal holds the original and the saved file only the terms, with a live quote. Reopened `resume` (opening refused as pending), then `fund`: every submission is the original, the wallet history has one transaction and there is one registration. ACK and ADM fixtures restore a journal-only prepared original before a first `fund`; ADM `native_lost_fresh_acknowledgement_recovers_after_provider_and_client_reopen` asserts that the registration request carries exactly that raw and the journal rows are unchanged.
- *Gap, filled.* No test entered through repeated `fund` with saved terms, after quote expiry, or checked the repaired copy when registration is then refused. Mutation M1 below (the expiry guard ignores a journaled original) passes every pre-existing consumer and chain test. New `native_journal_commit_before_local_copy_recovers_only_that_original` fails under it.

**3. Expired terms with no preparation.**

- *Spec.* R: "An expired agreement with neither saved nor journaled preparation SHALL NOT initiate a new payment." Scenario *Expired unaccepted preparation is not new funding authority*, first condition.
- *Existing.* `expired_unaccepted_quotes_never_broadcast_or_replace_preparation` (unprepared case): two `resume` refusals (`is_err`), no submission, no provider acceptance and an empty journal.
- *Gap, filled.* Fresh and repeated `fund` were untested, and neither the exact refusal nor the absence of chain and provider requests was asserted. New `native_expired_terms_create_no_payment_and_keep_an_unaccepted_original`, first part.
- *Review gap, filled.* Every expired fixture quote ends one second before the fixture starts, so the guard's exact edge was untested: review mutation R23 (`<` for `<=`, so unprepared terms count as live at exactly `expires_at`) passed all 100 consumer and chain tests, including the five new tests listed below. Under R23b, its equivalent after the seam below, recovery at `expires_at` prepares, registers, submits and opens a new payment, because the wall-clock provider still accepts. A `cfg(test)`-only clock, `opening_expiry_now`, now feeds this guard alone. New `native_unprepared_terms_are_refused_exactly_at_quote_expiry` fails under R23b.

**4. Expired prepared original: an earlier acknowledgement may still be requested.**

Scenario *Crash checkpoints*: "acknowledgement recovery precedes any permitted original submission, including after quote expiry".
- *Existing.* Checkpoint 1 of `funding_crash_boundaries_…` (quote expired at wall time, provider acceptance registered at `issued_at + 1`, saved raw without acknowledgement): one registration, every submission the original, one history transaction. Task 2.7 accepts first receipt at a pinned receiver clock equal to quote expiry. ADM `native_accepted_original_recovers_after_expiry_restart_and_refill_until_safe_credit` (2.3.4.3) recovers the real router's acknowledgement after expiry, restart and refill and records one submission, `[raw]`, with unchanged sender, operation, nonce, hash and raw columns.

**5. Expired prepared original without provider acceptance.**

**7. Fee estimates, the node's nonce and time do not re-sign; reopening rejects changed terms.**

- *Spec.* P: "Retrieving a prepared original SHALL NOT sign another transaction merely because time, fee estimates or the node's nonce changed"; scenario *Preparation is durable without submission*: "reopening the journal returns that original for the same operation and rejects changed recipient, value or commitment".
- *Existing.* Time: checkpoints 1–4 and the cell-2/3 tests use quotes that expired at wall time, and task 2.7 pins the receiver clock. - *Existing.* `resumed_open_accounts_preserve_exported_credentials_and_refresh_funding_status` (paid and free): reopened `resume` and `fund` return the provider's `frozen` refresh and the same `api_key`. `explicit_credential_issue_recovers_default_without_rotating_on_resume`: after an explicit local revocation, `resume` leaves export refusing (`explicitly issue`); an explicitly issued default then survives a reopened `resume`, with one `open` event and one history transaction. New `native_saved_account_recovery_refreshes_without_reopening_or_replacing_credentials`.
- *Review gap, filled.* As first written, that test stored only one credential, so a silent local reselection of another stored credential could not be observed. The test now also stores a spare inference-capable credential, explicitly issued while the default was valid. A lost first opening reply, whose account and credential were never saved, is owner-control's case. OCC's lost free opening is that case for free terms and is cited above only for its later saved-account refresh. #### Observation, not a code difference

Production already behaves as specified in every cell, so no production behaviour changed and no `Fixed` entry is needed. #### Limits

- The in-crate provider is a mock. It caches one acceptance and does not check sender nonces, so the nonce cell models an acceptance registered before the nonce moved. Credential expiry and provider revocation are fixture state, not elapsed time or a real revocation route.
- Accepted-at-safe is the fixture's scripted receipt and safe frontier, not live chain evidence.
- A recovered nonce conflict that later gains its canonical receipt is chain-level evidence only (`opening_prepare_recovers_nonce_conflict_after_canonical_receipt_returns`): the in-crate opening fixture requires a recorded submission before it opens.
- Only recovery's expiry guard is pinned. The exact edge of fresh `fund`'s validity check (`verify_quote`) and of the provider's own quote expiry are not proved here.
- A provider-side revocation is not consulted by native recovery. The test shows that a revoked default is neither replaced nor reselected, not that recovery reports the revocation.

#### Task 2.5.3 seam

The blocker was real: `resume_inner` makes the acknowledgement write, the second verification and the start write synchronously, with no await between them, so neither a provider reply nor another task can act there. [`consumer.rs`](../../../src-tauri/src/consumer.rs) is tracked and owner-authorised (APAS report line 29), so a test-only seam is allowed. All of it is `#[cfg(test)]`:

- the thread-local `BEFORE_BROADCAST_START_WRITE`, an optional boxed closure, and `before_broadcast_start_write()`, which runs it;
- one statement, `#[cfg(test)] before_broadcast_start_write();`, inside `if !saved.funding_broadcast_started`, after the marker is set in memory and immediately before `self.save_account(&saved)?`.

Non-test builds drop the statement and both items, so the production path is unchanged. The test guard `BroadcastStartWriteHook` asserts a current-thread runtime, refuses double arming and clears the hook on drop. Integration targets link the non-test library and cannot reach it. `nm` finds no `before_broadcast_start_write`, `BEFORE_BROADCAST_START_WRITE` or `BroadcastStartWriteHook` symbol in the built `chiral`, `chiral_daemon`, `relay_server` or `chiral_rpc_gateway`; the lib test binary has both seam symbols.

The failure is genuine. The hook applies task 2.5.2's obstruction (the account file is renamed aside and an empty directory takes its path), so the start write fails in `private_write` at its real file-to-directory rename with `cannot commit consumer state`, and its temporary is removed. No error value is injected, and no sleep or watcher is used. The in-crate fixture also gains `obstruct_on_delivery`, which applies the same obstruction once, before the mock provider delivers its acknowledgement.

#### New tests

All five are in `inference_funding_admission_test` and reuse its `Fixture`. The fixture gained three things. `ChainView` has chain-ID offset, syncing and frontier-age fields, and after review also another-genesis, safe-only age and signed head-timestamp offset fields; their defaults are the earlier healthy view, and the one older literal now fills them from `ChainView::default()`. `FundingGate::Qualified` toggles the development model in the qualified set. - `fresh_intent_owner_refusals_precede_chain_observation_with_every_gate_open`. For each envelope, with every gate open, healthy RPC, ample balance and a current nonce, it sends the expired, missing, wrong-signer, other-body, other-target and other-wallet-own-payment cases, and (added by review) the owner's valid proof for a never-issued quote. Each is refused (401, 403 for the other wallet, 404 for the never-issued quote) with no RPC request, no intent row and no paid accounting. The quoted owner's fresh proof is then acknowledged, and its replay is refused with no RPC and an unchanged acknowledgement. Catches an owner proof accepted without its lifetime, signature, body, target or quoted-owner binding, and authority checked only after chain observation.
- `nonce_refusals_keep_the_earlier_acknowledgement_and_admit_the_unchanged_next_original`. For each envelope, an acknowledged unbroadcast first agreement at nonce 0, then a second quote of the same owner. The second agreement is refused four times at the nonce observation: next nonce while the first is unbroadcast and while it is pending (400), nonce 0 while pending (409 `funding_pending`) and once spent (400). The first acknowledgement is recovered unchanged with no RPC, and neither agreement has an account. Catches a nonce refusal that disturbs earlier accepted evidence, and an earlier acknowledgement that blocks the sender's next nonce.
- `fresh_intent_requires_identity_checked_healthy_chain_evidence_before_and_after_balance`. Eight unhealthy views per envelope: another chain ID, a syncing node, the safe frontier above the head, a head and frontier aged 600 seconds, and (added by review) a safe frontier aged 400 seconds behind a fresh head, a head 600 seconds ahead of the local clock, a safe timestamp later than the head's, and a safe frontier at the head's height with another hash. - `fresh_intent_admits_type_two_priority_fee_equal_to_its_signed_maximum`. A type-2 original with priority equal to its maximum of 11 is refused one wei below full cost at the balance observation and acknowledged at exact cost. Catches a decoder that refuses equality.

#### Observation, not a code difference

At fresh admission, a configured-RPC chain-ID or genesis mismatch is refused with 400 `invalid_payment`, because `ChainClient::identity_check` returns `Rejected` and the route maps that to a payment refusal. This specification requires only that unverified identity not admit, and it does not. The pending `specify-funding-settlement` change records the same classification as its code difference 4: untrustworthy chain evidence should be unavailable, not a payment rejection. The new health test asserts today's code, and its comment names that change; reclassifying would change that expectation. No code difference was found against this specification, and no production code changed.

### Ledger tests

`full_paid_opening_capacity_leaves_existing_requests_and_inference_gates_intact`, `full_owned_free_opening_capacity_leaves_existing_requests_and_inference_gates_intact` and `full_global_free_opening_capacity_leaves_existing_requests_and_inference_gates_intact` run one case body, `opening_capacity_case`, once per resource, at explicit logical times. The owner's paid account A (995 wei available, one wei per token) holds the existing requests. `OpeningPressure` quotes every opening at 950 and takes every unit of the resource but the last. The last unit is taken genuinely at 1,000:

- **Paid.** 9,999 complete signed restored unopened agreements, each with its own quote, distinct payer, opaque original, signed acknowledgement and admission lease. Then a genuine registration takes the 10,000th lease.
- **Owned free.** A and six genuine free openings by the owner, then a seventh genuine opening: eight accounts.
- **Global free.** A, a genuine free opening by another owner and 9,997 complete signed restored free accounts with root credentials and distinct owners. Then the owner's genuine free opening makes 10,000 accounts. No owner has more than two.

Fullness is proved, not assumed. A fresh opening of that kind is refused with exactly `rate_limit: active funding intent capacity reached` or `rate_limit: free account limit reached`, and changes no row. The fresh opening is the owner's paid funding-intent registration, or the owner's first free opening; for the ledger bound, also the first free opening of an owner with no account. The check runs right after the fill and again after every later step, and also right after reopening, before restart recovery runs. So the resource is full whenever an inference operation runs, and nothing that follows frees capacity for an opening. The fixture's own counts state each bound exactly.

Three 30-wei holds are reserved before the fill and four while the resource is full. While full:

- one hold from each period settles at known (10, 7) usage for 17 wei;
- one from each period releases as unknown usage, returning the whole 30 wei with 30 wei of exposure, and one more releases as invalid (10, 21) usage;
- reads by key and by opaque identifier, the request list and a signed receipt bind exactly the stored records, and only the receipt sequence changes;
- a fresh request is admitted and charged 15 wei. Requests beyond the credit, by a `read`-only credential, on a held slot, with changed serving terms and with held keys are each refused by their own rule, with every row unchanged.

The store is then reopened. Restart recovery runs before any inference admission and releases the two remaining holds, one from each period. The freed slot is admitted again and charged 17 wei. Late outcomes return the first results. A ends at 927 wei available, 68 spent, (41, 27) tokens, with its 5-wei fee and 1,000 funded. Complete rows are compared with fixed literals throughout. The capacity state equals its state at the fill: restored rows, leases, quotes, accounts, funding, fees, rate limits and metadata.

Two controls show the refusals are the full resource's. The refused paid original is admitted unchanged once every filling lease has ended. With the owner's bound full, another owner's first free opening is admitted. The ledger bound has no such control, because accounts are permanent.

Restored rows are compared exactly as their undecoded text (`CapacityRows`), so no step decodes 10,000 records. The paid case takes about 5 seconds, the ledger bound about 9.

### Limits

- Restored leases and accounts are complete signed storage prerequisites, not observed admissions or openings. Their raw originals are opaque ledger inputs after the chain-verification boundary.
- Paid account A's opening credit is post-verification funding. Usage is supplied, and the controlled engine is test evidence, not llama.cpp, chain or hardware evidence.
- Authorization here is credential scope, concurrency and bearer reads. No owner-proof read runs at full opening capacity. Owner-proof request reads are covered by `request_accounting_and_receipts_recover_at_full_quote_storage`, at full quote storage only. Inference settlement, restart recovery and bearer reads need no owner proof. The challenge budget is not exhausted.
- Process-kill and fsync durability and daemon startup ordering are not established.
- This does not cover native recovery of a lost first paid opening reply (*Acceptance gaps and evidence limits* below).

With these tests every cell of request-accounting's reconciliation matrix names a test, and parent 2.8.2 is complete.
