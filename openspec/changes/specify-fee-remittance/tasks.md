## 2. Close code differences

- [x] 2.1 Report the four book states in the fee view (requirements 3 and 11, design code difference 1). In `src-tauri/src/billing_store.rs` `fee_summary`, derive each obligation's reported state from its stored status and its batch's status, using the table in design decision 4: `pending` → pending; `batched` with batch `prepared` → batched; `batched` with batch `submitted` or `orphaned` → submitted; `remitted` → confirmed. Report `cancelled` obligations as cancelled. Report `orphaned_batched` and `orphaned_remitted` obligations as orphaned, split by the same batch-state mapping, and keep them out of the ordinary totals. Keep exact `U256` sums as canonical decimal strings. Do not rename stored statuses; `inference-funding-settlement` restoration depends on them. Update the assertions on `amounts_wei.remitted` and `amounts_wei.batched` in `fee_obligation_cannot_be_marked_paid_without_matching_chain_evidence` and `orphan_restoration_compensates_spending_and_every_fee_state_once_across_restart`. Regression, which fails without the change: a ledger test with ordinary obligations of 5 wei pending, 7 wei in a `prepared` batch, 11 wei in a `submitted` batch and 13 wei in an `accepted` batch reports pending 5, batched 7, submitted 11 and confirmed 13.
  Done 2026-11-18: `BillingStore::fee_summary` joins each obligation to its batch and reports ordinary obligations in `amounts_wei` as `pending`, `batched`, `submitted` and `confirmed` by the decision 4 table (`batched` with a `prepared` batch is batched; with a `submitted` or `orphaned` batch, submitted; `remitted` with an `accepted` batch, confirmed). `cancelled` obligations are totalled in `cancelled_wei`, and `orphaned_batched` and `orphaned_remitted` obligations in `orphaned_amounts_wei` by the same batch-state mapping, outside `amounts_wei`. Every total is an exact `U256` decimal string, zeros included. A stored combination that the ledger's transitions never produce (for example `remitted` with an unaccepted batch, or a missing batch) fails the view as a ledger invariant instead of being reported under a guessed state. Stored statuses are unchanged. Regression `fee_view_reports_the_four_book_states_from_obligation_and_batch_state` (5 pending, 7 in a `prepared` batch, 11 in a `submitted` batch, 13 in an `accepted` batch: pending 5, batched 7, submitted 11, confirmed 13, with the stored statuses still `pending`, `batched` and `remitted`) fails without the change (`batched` 18, `remitted` 13, no `submitted`). The assertions on `amounts_wei.remitted` and `amounts_wei.batched` now read `confirmed` 5 and, after the remittance is proven orphaned, `submitted` 5 (batched 0) in `fee_obligation_cannot_be_marked_paid_without_matching_chain_evidence`, and the reported name (`remitted` → `confirmed`) in `orphan_restoration_compensates_spending_and_every_fee_state_once_across_restart`, which now also shows that while the funding is orphaned the fee appears only as cancelled 5 (pending case) or orphaned 5 under its batch state, never in `amounts_wei`.

Beside `collection` it adds `platform_receipt`: "A recorded fee obligation is not proof that the platform received the fee." Regression `app::tests::fee_view_lists_confirmed_remittances_and_the_receipt_statement` (operator bearer, no `Origin`, both routes, after a confirmed 5-wei batch in block 120: the batch with its transaction hash and block evidence, the statement, and no `raw_transaction` key or signed-bytes hex in the response) fails without the change: with the batch list and statement removed, with only the statement removed, and with the stored batch records listed in place of the view (the signed bytes leak). `fee_obligation_cannot_be_marked_paid_without_matching_chain_evidence` also checks that an orphaned remittance is listed as submitted with its transaction hash and no inclusion. Regression `fee_view_lists_only_the_newest_100_batches_but_totals_every_obligation` (101 sealed 5-wei batches: exactly the newest 100 listed newest first, the first sealed omitted, while `batched` still totals 505) fails without the change (no `batches`), without the `LIMIT 100` bound and with oldest-first order. - [x] 2.3 Evaluate and seal over every pending obligation (requirements 4 and 6, design code difference 3). In `src-tauri/src/billing_store.rs` `prepare_fee_batches`, compute each platform wallet's pending total, with checked exact arithmetic, and its oldest creation time over every pending obligation, not only the 1,000 oldest. For example, stream the pending rows in chunks inside the same ledger transaction. When either threshold holds, or `force` is set, seal every pending obligation of that wallet in that transaction. Split a wallet into several batches only at a work bound or a `u128` overflow. Keep the per-pass work bounded in the number of batches submitted. Regressions, which fail without the change:
  - 1,200 pending obligations of 900,000,000,000 wei to one wallet, all created at the same time 3,600 seconds before `now`, are all sealed by one unforced call;
  - a forced call with 1,001 pending 5-wei obligations leaves none pending.
  Done 2026-11-17: `prepare_fee_batches` sums each platform wallet's pending obligations as a checked `U256` total, with their oldest acceptance time, over every pending row, then `seal_fee_wallet` seals every pending obligation of each qualifying (or forced) wallet in the same transaction, reading keyset chunks of 1,000 and starting a new batch only where a `u128` transfer value would overflow; it fails the transaction unless the sealed members equal the decided total. No per-obligation work bound; at most 100 unconfirmed batches are returned. Regressions `amount_threshold_is_evaluated_and_sealed_over_every_pending_obligation` (1,200 × 900,000,000,000 wei, 3,600 seconds old: one 1,080,000,000,000,000-wei batch, another wallet's 999,999,999,999,999 wei left pending) and `explicit_remittance_seals_every_pending_obligation_but_never_a_cancelled_one` (1,001 × 5 wei plus another wallet: none pending, the cancelled obligation unsealed) fail without the change (0 batches; 3 left pending). `a_wallet_splits_into_several_batches_only_at_u128_overflow` covers the split.

## 3. Acceptance evidence per requirement

Task 3.N corresponds to requirement N in the spec and to row N of the design coverage table. A task is checked only when existing passing tests prove every statement and scenario of its requirement. An unchecked task names the missing case.

- [ ] 3.1 The fee is a funding charge, never a request charge. Existing evidence: `full_width_money_rounds_once_without_float_or_sqlite_integer` (fees 0 and 1 for 199 and 200 wei), `open_replay_preserves_credentials_holds_and_spend_without_crediting_twice` (fee 5 with 150 spent and a held request), `performance_waiver_preserves_usage_and_releases_reservation_exactly_once` (fee 5 after a waiver), `free_sessions_authenticate_without_funding_or_fee_evidence`, `pending_capacity_rejects_new_intents_without_losing_accepted_obligations` and `concurrent_openings_issue_only_one_initial_secret_and_credit`. Missing: a ledger test in which a 199-wei top-up records no `fee_obligations` row and a later 200-wei top-up records exactly one 1-wei row.
- [ ] 3.2 Each nonzero fee creates one durable obligation owed to the agreement's platform wallet. Existing evidence: `paid_topup_recovers_at_full_paid_intent_capacity_without_fresh_admission` (exact obligation row with the agreement's platform wallet), `orphan_restoration_compensates_spending_and_every_fee_state_once_across_restart` (a pending obligation survives reopening) and `open_replay_preserves_credentials_holds_and_spend_without_crediting_twice`. Missing: a provider test that restarts with a different configured `platform_wallet` and shows the earlier obligation still owed and later batched to the agreement's wallet, while a new agreement's obligation is owed to the new wallet. Also missing: a test that a drain, offer expiry and restart leave a pending obligation unchanged.
- [ ] 3.3 Obligations move only forward through the four states. Existing evidence: `fee_obligation_cannot_be_marked_paid_without_matching_chain_evidence` and `fee_batches_survive_restart_and_have_immutable_submission_identity`. Missing, besides task 2.1: a contract-level test with an RPC fixture that walks one obligation through batched, submitted and confirmed as reported by the fee view, and shows that a failed submission and a restart never return it to pending or to a second batch.
- [ ] 3.4 Automatic aggregation seals at 10^15 wei or 86,400 seconds. Existing evidence: `fee_batches_survive_restart_and_have_immutable_submission_identity` (the inclusive age bound at 86,400 seconds). Missing, besides task 2.3: ledger tests for 999,999,999,999,999 wei (no batch) and then 10^15 wei (one batch of exactly that amount), a 5-wei obligation at 86,399 seconds (not sealed), and 600,000,000,000,000 wei owed to each of two wallets (no batch; then only the older wallet sealed). Also missing: a maintenance test in which a drained provider seals and submits a batch at 86,400 seconds.
- [ ] 3.5 A sealed batch is immutable and pays the exact sum. Existing evidence: `fee_batches_survive_restart_and_have_immutable_submission_identity`, `open_replay_preserves_credentials_holds_and_spend_without_crediting_twice`, `orphan_credit_freezes_reverses_and_records_consumed_shortfall`, `orphan_restoration_compensates_spending_and_every_fee_state_once_across_restart` and `restoring_one_funding_never_unfreezes_other_pending_or_orphaned_funding`. Missing: a ledger test sealing 5-wei and 7-wei obligations into one 12-wei batch whose members belong to no other batch, and `update_fee_batch` refusals for a changed platform wallet and for changed signed bytes.
- [ ] 3.6 Explicit remittance is a local operator action on the loopback administration listener. Existing evidence: none. Missing, besides task 2.3:
  - administration-router tests in which `POST /admin/fees/remit` with the bearer seals and submits a 5-wei obligation 100 seconds old;
  - refusals with HTTP 401 for an `Origin` header, a missing bearer and a consumer account bearer, each sealing nothing;
  - a public-router test in which `/admin/fees` and `/admin/fees/remit` answer 404;
  - a `provider_config` test refusing a non-loopback or zero administration port;
  - a test that `GET /admin/fees` changes no state;
  - a test that a gas-starved `fees/remit` answers HTTP 500 `internal_error` without RPC content, while `GET /admin/status` shows the insufficient-balance category as `maintenance.fee_error`;
  - `scripts/provider-admin.py` tests refusing a 0644 token file before any request and sending `POST` to `127.0.0.1` with proxies disabled.
- [ ] 3.7 Each batch is remitted by one provider-wallet transfer through the shared journal, and the provider pays the gas. Existing evidence: `rpc_outage_fee_gas_failure_and_reorg_keep_distinct_admission_states`, `shared_base_serializes_concurrent_consumer_preparation_and_provider_fee`, `durable_intent_restart_and_nonce_serialization` and `cross_process_wallet_lock_is_exclusive`. Missing: a contract-level test of a successful remittance that decodes the journaled signed bytes (provider sender, platform recipient, 5-wei value, calldata committing to the batch identifier) and shows every account unchanged. Also missing: a test that a busy wallet defers remittance and then remits after the other original resolves, and an assertion that `funding_ready` is not closed by a gas failure.
- [ ] 3.8 A remittance is confirmed only under the funding settlement policy. Existing evidence: `fee_obligation_cannot_be_marked_paid_without_matching_chain_evidence` and `legacy_and_typed_execution_waits_for_safe_not_first_receipt_or_finalized`. Missing: `update_fee_batch` refusals for evidence with a sender other than the provider wallet, another recipient, other calldata or another network, and a contract-level test in which a remittance receipt above the `safe` frontier leaves the batch submitted until two consistent observations at `safe`.
- [ ] 3.9 Confirmed remittances are monitored, and a reorganization never causes a second transfer. Existing evidence: `fee_obligation_cannot_be_marked_paid_without_matching_chain_evidence`, `canonical_receipts_safe_and_reorg_boundaries`, `exact_canonical_hash_failure_and_orphan_have_distinct_outcomes` and `reincluded_identical_payment_refreshes_journal_evidence_without_broadcast`. Missing, besides task 2.4: `contracts::reconcile` tests with an RPC fixture in which a confirmed batch observed pending returns to submitted, a proven orphan rebroadcasts the identical bytes and is confirmed again with new block evidence while the journal keeps one record for the batch, and an RPC outage leaves the batch confirmed with a fee failure.
- [ ] 3.10 Restart and ambiguity recover the original remittance without replacement. Existing evidence: `durable_intent_restart_and_nonce_serialization`, `ambiguous_broadcast_retains_original_evidence`, `fee_batches_survive_restart_and_have_immutable_submission_identity` and `nonce_regression_after_reconciliation_never_signs_a_competing_original`. Missing: a contract-level test in which the remittance is journaled but the ledger never recorded it with its batch, and a restart resubmits the same bytes and records them. Also missing: tests in which a remittance whose execution failed at `safe`, or whose nonce was consumed without its receipt, stays unconfirmed with no new transaction while each pass reports a fee failure.
- [ ] 3.11 The operator fee view separates obligations from actual remittances. Existing evidence: `fee_obligation_cannot_be_marked_paid_without_matching_chain_evidence`, `orphan_restoration_compensates_spending_and_every_fee_state_once_across_restart` and `restoring_one_funding_never_unfreezes_other_pending_or_orphaned_funding`. Missing: the regressions of tasks 2.1 and 2.2, plus a test that a cancelled 5-wei obligation and an orphaned 7-wei obligation in a submitted batch appear only in their separate totals.
- [ ] 3.12 Fee collection is provider-remitted and not trustless. Existing evidence: `rpc_outage_fee_gas_failure_and_reorg_keep_distinct_admission_states` (the account stays active after a fee failure). Missing: a provider test in which a remittance fails on every pass while the account's status and balances and `funding_ready` stay unchanged, and the regression of task 2.2 for the receipt disclaimer.
