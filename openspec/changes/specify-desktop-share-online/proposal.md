## Why

The book's adopted target design makes the desktop a local LLM client that can **share** a loaded model online: the desktop turns it into a provider deployment that other people pay to use (Part I "Desktop product: a local client that can share"; Part II "Desktop local-client workflow (target design)", *Share online*). Milestone 4 of that workflow is "the share wizard with the launch window, automatic qualification, Auto-price and the provider dashboard". It needs its own OpenSpec change, tests and release gates before it is presented as implemented.

Today no specification covers sharing. The desktop can only reach an externally operated provider through the loopback operator dashboard (Appendix A.5.5). Provisioning and qualification are operator procedures run with Python scripts (`docs/provider-qualification.md`). The Quit confirmation (`desktop-window-lifecycle`) knows nothing about provider obligations. Without requirements, an implementation could:

- publish unqualified paid generation;
- spend qualification funds without approval, or disturb a qualification measurement with live traffic;
- change prices on missing or fabricated reputation evidence, or alter accepted agreements when prices change;
- expose administration, the engine or an unqualified calibration gateway beyond loopback;
- put the administrator token in the webview;
- give one wallet two nonce journals;
- hide prepaid liabilities, or automatic fee remittance, from the user;
- leave a provider daemon orphaned, or silently stop it, when the window closes or the app quits.

## What Changes

- Add a six-step **Share wizard** (choose, provider identity, reachability, qualification, pricing, publish) on a new `#/share` page with its navigation item. Steps run in order, and changing an earlier choice invalidates later results. Every new share is first published free, in a **launch window**, and is priced later by the **Auto-price** checkbox, on by default, or manually (book *Author decisions* items 8–11, the 2026-11-16 revision that replaces the earlier items 8 and 9). Sharing never starts automatically, and the browser preview fabricates nothing.
- **Choose:** only installed, verified models (milestone 1) with exact serving settings, fit-checked with the book's headroom rules. Qualification eligibility is checked before anything is spent. The untrusted-GGUF risk is disclosed before an installation is first shared. The engine is handed over to the provider daemon after confirmation, and the model is never loaded twice.
- **Provider identity:**
  - an explicitly selected provider profile with an enumerated, strict field set and the same network identity as the consumer profile, now including a nullable `catalog_url` from which Auto-price and the dashboard read the launch reputation;
  - the provider wallet may be the consumer wallet;
  - each signing wallet keeps one recorded `data_dir` base, and conflicting profile loads or daemon starts are refused;
  - one published deployment per network and provider wallet, with its own persisted DHT identity, reused rather than replaced. Accounts a new model will not serve are listed and acknowledged before publishing.
- **Gas:** the verified provider-wallet balance and a remittance-gas estimate are shown, with a statement that the daemon remits automatically. The app never uses a faucet automatically, and publishing is blocked when gas is insufficient, for free shares too (whether a free share without fee obligations may skip gas is design.md open question 12).
- **Reachability:** libp2p only (book *Author decisions* item 7, decided 2026-11-16): direct, hole-punched or relayed. The wizard offers no HTTPS origin and accepts no HTTP endpoint; a provider that wants an HTTPS origin runs `chiral_daemon` with the provider guide and its preflight, and an HTTPS option with an external probe may return in a later change. Every share therefore publishes schema v2 offers declaring only `libp2p`, with a `null` endpoint, and nothing can be published until milestone 3 is implemented. A share uses the 1–32 relays its provider profile lists: the project's serving-capable testnet relays named in shipped profiles, or relays anyone else operates, with no relay fee (book *Author decisions* item 2). The connection is verified through a relay with an identity binding. The first check, before any qualification spending, runs in a new daemon reachability-check mode under the identity that will publish. The second runs with the drained published deployment before publishing.
- **In-app qualification**, needed before any generation share is priced, runs within a budget approved in the qualification step, computed exactly from the runner's request counts:
  - the paying wallet must differ from the provider wallet;
  - the budget is a hard cap with no automatic top-ups, and the exact funding order and *Resume* recovery apply;
  - an approval authorizes exactly one run and lapses unspent when the share stops or its settings change;
  - a calibration-only consumer context is used, leaving the loaded profile's origin rules unchanged, and every calibration listener stays on loopback, on ports distinct from the published deployment's;
  - evidence matches `scripts/qualify-provider.py` exactly, with the request journal written before dispatch and ambiguous requests reconciled before a retry;
  - a run can be cancelled before it starts, or stopped with *Stop qualification*;
  - a failed class is never published, and calibration state is preserved;
  - *Share without qualification* publishes the share free and unqualified, and the budget can be approved later on the dashboard.
- **Automatic qualification in the first quiet hour** (book *Share online* step 4). The approved run starts after a one-hour warm-up, at the first moment with no request admitted for 15 minutes and none in flight, or 12 hours after publication unless a stated deferral reason holds, or at once with *Qualify now*; it is offered only while the share's model has no serving profile. The published deployment enters a **qualification pause**: it withdraws its offers, answers consumers with a retryable HTTP 503 `qualification_in_progress` and a `Retry-After` of 1–3,600 seconds, which native consumers treat as not admitted and never retry automatically as a paid request (`specify-p2p-provider-transport`), lets in-flight requests finish and stops its engine, while keeping its identity, reservations, ledger, fee remittance and recovery routes (status, receipts, cancellation, crediting acknowledged funding). The calibration deployment then measures. A failed or stopped run relaunches the engine, and the share stays free and retryable with a new approval. The pause counts as unavailable in the launch reputation, with no maintenance exemption, so a run of D minutes leaves 72 − D minutes of other unavailability within the 95% ready-to-charge bar over the first 24 hours; the wizard says so.
- **Promotion in place.** A passing run's qualified ModelSpec is promoted into the existing published deployment by a graceful restart: same wallet, peer ID, ledger and revision counter, then revalidation, readiness and the final reachability check before resuming. From then on the model keeps its serving profile at every price, free included, so price changes never change execution terms, and catalogs can see the valid qualification that ready-to-charge needs. Free accounts opened under the earlier unqualified offers keep being served after it under their allowance, with the same model and settings and no speed promise (book *Share online* step 4; design.md open question 15, decided 2026-11-16), through a widened execution-compatibility rule in the MODIFIED `inference-commercial-terms` requirement *Existing agreements permit only evidence-only renewal of identical terms*. A failed promotion stays drained, with retry or restore offered, never automatically.
- **Free shares** (book *Author decisions* item 1, decided 2026-11-16): a generation share whose input price, output price and minimum funding are all zero may skip qualification. It needs no paying wallet or budget, publishes with `serving: null` after the daemon's readiness probes (exact tokenizer and template identity, capability probes and a real inference with usage), carries no speed class or waiver, and is labeled *Unqualified — no speed guarantee* on the Share page and on every consumer surface of the desktop. No nonzero price or minimum funding is published for a generation share without a valid qualification, by Auto-price or by hand. A free share still needs remittance gas to publish, as the book's step 2 requires of every share.
- **Launch window** (book *Author decisions* items 8 and 11): every new share is first published free, with the free allowance, and a new generation share without a serving profile. With Auto-price on, it stays free for at least 24 hours and until a review finds the catalog reporting it ready to charge, whatever its floor. A new share inherits no earlier share's prices, qualification, budget approval or Auto-price settings. *Share again* on a stopped share is not a new share: it offers that share's accepted terms and Auto-price state unchanged, so a stopped paid share's prepaid accounts can be served again.
- **Free allowance** (book *Author decisions* item 10): every free offer signs `specify-p2p-provider-transport`'s per-account `free_allowance`, preset to 20,000 tokens and 24 hours and adjustable within that change's bounds.
- **Auto-price** (book *Author decisions* items 8 and 11), a checkbox on by default: the user sets a target price and a floor (default free); every 6 hours, never sooner across restarts, the desktop moves the price at most one step among 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100% of the target, with exact integer rounding, never below the floor after the launch window. It moves **up** when the catalog reports the provider ready to charge (now while free, or at the end of its latest free period while charging), its service score is at least 90 and it has a valid qualification; **down** when the service score is below 80, the 14-day availability below 95% or the payment-gated score below 45 with at least 5 votes in 14 days, down taking precedence; and **holds** otherwise. Inputs come only from the provider profile's catalog; an unavailable or stale input holds the price and is never replaced by an estimate. The dashboard shows the step prices, current step, next review, each decision with its inputs and reason, and a local history. Unchecking it keeps the current price and switches to manual pricing.
- **Manual pricing and repricing in place:** with Auto-price off, prices change only by the user's confirmed action, and a nonzero generation price needs a valid qualification; the launch reputation is a signal, not a gate. Every price change, automatic or manual, is a new signed offer revision published by a new daemon repricing action, without draining or restarting the engine; accepted agreements and signed quotes keep their terms.
- **Embedding shares** need readiness but no speed class. They are also first published free. Because the book's *ready to charge* requires "a valid qualification", which an embedding share cannot hold, an embedding share is never ready to charge: Auto-price is unavailable for it, with that reason shown, and it has no launch window. Manual pricing, whose nonzero input price needs no qualification, remains (design.md decision 44 and open question 16, revised 2026-11-17).
- **Pricing:**
  - the Auto-price target and floor, or a manual price, and the free allowance, entered in the pricing step and later on the dashboard;
  - exact CFX-to-wei conversion, with the fixed 0.5% fee shown as exact amounts;
  - a free price has exactly zero minimum funding;
  - published prices are independent of the calibration rates, because Auto-price changes them without new qualification (design.md open question 8, decided by book items 8 and 11; the earlier interim rule is withdrawn).
- **Publish** shows the exact terms, the pricing mode and the free allowance, and requires an accepted disclosure. The disclosure states, for every share, in the book's words, that "consumers will prepay non-refundable balances that only this machine can serve", adding that this applies once the share charges, and that free accounts opened during free periods prepay nothing and are served within the free allowance (and, without a serving profile, that no speed is guaranteed); it also covers the launch window, the automatic qualification with its pause and availability cost, the promotion, after which free accounts opened before it keep being served under their allowance (decision 45), automatic price changes with Auto-price on, offline behavior, plaintext prompts, the advertised public IP address, reputation, fees, automatic remittance and gas, attribution, model licensing and relay metadata. Following book *Author decisions* item 9 and step 6, the reputation statement says that only paying users' feedback counts, so free service earns no ratings, and that catalogs observe this machine's availability and, while it is free, probe it and publish a service score (the launch reputation of `specify-provider-launch-reputation`), shown beside the payment-gated score, never blended into it, and attached to the provider wallet. Publication is confirmed from the daemon's published-offer list.
- **Managed `chiral_daemon`:**
  - only the daemon bundled with the desktop, as a child process with loopback administration, engine and HTTP listeners, a non-loopback DHT listener for the published deployment, no advertised HTTPS origin, and a complete generated `libp2p_serving` block;
  - protected tokens read natively and a minimal environment that keeps the desktop's policy authority;
  - a natively verified signed version policy for every daemon, calibration included;
  - it starts without publishing until resumed;
  - two new operator-only administration actions: the qualification pause and its end, and repricing a running model by a new offer revision;
  - the desktop refuses occupied ports or held locks, binds the child to its own lifetime, detects a leftover daemon, and never restarts it automatically.
- **Share dashboard:** the **Free** or **Paid** mode, the pricing mode and Auto-price state and history, the launch window, the qualification state, readiness versus funding readiness, the withdrawal latch, the libp2p path, offers, accounts and exact liabilities, request metadata, fees with the automatic-remittance thresholds, earnings and gas. It offers drain, resume, refresh, *Remit now* with confirmation, the qualification approval, *Qualify now*, cancel and stop, and the pricing controls. It shows the provider's launch reputation and payment-gated reputation exactly as the provider profile's catalog reports them, and never computes them. On a relayed path, drain, the dashboard while no offer is current, *Stop sharing* and Quit give the relayed-grace statement (book *Author decisions* item 6, decided 2026-11-16). The statement says that while the daemon runs, serving relays keep carrying request status, receipts, cancellation and existing accounts until 24 hours after this machine's most recent offer verified by the relay expires. After that, consumers may lose access to them through the relay unless a direct or hole-punched path is established. A relay whose evidence store is full or has failed can end the grace earlier, because milestone 3 bounds a relay's durable grace evidence by an operator-configured relay-wide quota and evicts the record with the earliest grace end, and fails closed for grace when that store fails. The book counts the 24 hours from the signed `expires_at` of the most recent offer the relay itself verified, so the dashboard shows the last observed offer's `expires_at` and, 24 hours later, the latest time the grace can end.
- **Local use of a shared model** goes through admission on an operator-only loopback route, as milestone 1's carve-outs delegate. It takes a slot and, when the shared model has a serving profile, part of the qualified arrival budget at every price (a share without one has none), is never billed and is excluded from performance observations. During qualification, including the pause, local use of the model and any local load, including a load of the installation under qualification, are refused. While sharing, loading another model on overlapping devices needs an explicit acknowledgement.
- **Closing the window** keeps sharing running. Milestone 1's status bar shows the sharing state and path on every page. As the book requires, logout or a profile switch is refused while sharing or qualifying.
- **Sleep prevention** is automatic while sharing (book *Author decisions* item 4, decided 2026-11-16): the wizard asks for consent with the option preselected; with consent, idle system sleep is prevented whenever a share is active or a qualification run is in progress, and released when they stop. Consent is revocable in Settings or on the dashboard. Lid-close and user-initiated sleep are never blocked.
- **Stop sharing** shows liabilities, stops a running qualification first, then drains, waits for in-flight requests, shuts down gracefully and preserves all state, including Auto-price's settings and history. It forces no remittance unless the user chooses it.
- An active share pins its model and runtime against deletion, replacement and eject.
- Sharing keeps every secret native and adds no plugin permission to the webview.
- **Modified `desktop-window-lifecycle`:**
  - the Quit confirmation lists active shares and qualification runs, and gives the relayed-grace statement for a relayed share;
  - confirmed quitting stops them safely before `logout`;
  - an interceptable application quit (such as Cmd+Q) opens the confirmation while sharing or qualifying;
  - the tray tooltip shows the sharing state and path.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `desktop-share-online`: the desktop share wizard and Share page, provider profile, identity, wallet base and gas, libp2p-only reachability verification and the reachability-check mode, in-app qualification within an approved budget and its explicit stop, free and embedding shares published after readiness without speed qualification, the launch window in which every new share is first published free, the free allowance, automatic qualification in the first quiet hour with its pause and in-place promotion under the same provider wallet and peer ID, Auto-price from the catalog's launch reputation, manual pricing and repricing by new offer revisions, the *Unqualified — no speed guarantee* label on consumer surfaces, pricing and the publish disclosure with its ratings, launch-reputation and Auto-price statements, managed provider daemon and dashboard with the share's mode and the relayed-grace statement, local use through admission, competing-load rules, close/logout/sleep behavior, stop sharing, artifact pinning and the secret and permission boundary.

### Modified Capabilities

- `desktop-window-lifecycle`: **Quitting is an explicit, confirmed action** now lists active shares and qualification runs, gives the relayed-grace statement for a relayed share, stops them safely before closing the session, and routes interceptable application quit requests to the confirmation while sharing or qualifying. **The system tray offers Show and Quit** now shows the sharing state and path in its tooltip. The other requirements are unchanged.
- `inference-commercial-terms` (pending in `specify-inference-commercial-terms`, not yet in `openspec/specs/`): **Native deployment constraints cannot be bypassed by declaration validity** gains the free-generation exception of book *Author decisions* item 1: outside development, a generation model whose token rates are both zero may be configured and published with no serving profile after its readiness probes, claiming no speed class, capacity tier or qualification identity, with a minimum funding of `"0"`. A priced generation model without a serving profile is still rejected, and qualified and unprofiled models still never share one provider process. The rest of the requirement is copied unchanged.

## Impact

- **Dependencies:**
  - Milestone 1, `specify-desktop-local-models`, is required. It provides:
    - installed, verified artifacts (`chiral-artifact-sha256.json`, `chiral-model.json`) and pinned runtimes;
    - the model library, load/eject with fit checks, and local chat;
    - the carve-outs that delegate a shared installation's local generation to this change and refuse loading it locally.

    Context length is fixed by the installed ModelSpec, so a share's context is chosen when the model is installed. This change modifies none of milestone 1's requirements; it adds *Local use of a shared model passes through admission*, as milestone 1's design expects.
  - Milestone 3, `specify-p2p-provider-transport`, is required for every share, because desktop shares are libp2p-only (book *Author decisions* item 7). It provides libp2p reachability, schema v2 offers with a `null` endpoint published on `/chiral/inference/manifest/2` (with no v1 acceptance window, book *Author decisions* item 5), the connectivity path, relay facts, the `libp2p_serving` block, whose 1–32 relays and stream-server limits the desktop writes, the shipped profiles' project relay lists, the 24-hour relayed grace after offers lapse (book *Author decisions* item 6, recorded against its open question 3), and the per-account `free_allowance` that free offers sign and providers enforce (book *Author decisions* item 10), under which an Auto-price change is an ordinary new offer revision that alters no opened account. Until it is implemented, the wizard stops at the reachability step: no reachability check, no qualification budget request through the wizard and no publication, free or paid. A relayed share also needs a serving relay whose deployment has been separately authorized.
  - Milestone 2, `specify-desktop-local-api-server`, is optional: once it exists, its routes to a shared model go through the same admission.
  - Milestone 5, `specify-provider-launch-reputation` (renamed from `specify-provider-track-record`), is a **release dependency**. The book's *Status and milestones* requires it to exist before launch windows or Auto-price are presented as available, and every new share starts in a launch window by default, so sharing is not presented as available until it is implemented. Auto-price reads its service score, availability and ready-to-charge result (design.md decision 41 lists the facts it needs). It is not an archive-order dependency: this change names that change in the publish disclosure, the dashboard and Auto-price's inputs, but references none of its requirements or its capability ID, which are being revised.
  - The pending `specify-inference-commercial-terms` is required, because this change modifies its requirements *Native deployment constraints cannot be bypassed by declaration validity* (the free-generation exception) and *Existing agreements permit only evidence-only renewal of identical terms* (the pre-promotion free-agreement exception). Archive `specify-inference-commercial-terms` before this change. If that change's copy of either requirement changes before it is archived, rebase this change's MODIFIED copy onto the new text, keeping only this change's exception and its scenarios. The second exception relies on the signed `free_allowance` that `specify-p2p-provider-transport` adds, which is one more reason to archive that change first.
  - Archive this change after milestone 1, whose capabilities and carve-outs it references, after `specify-inference-commercial-terms`, and after `specify-p2p-provider-transport`, without whose implementation nothing can be published.

  - `inference-opening-funding-intent`, archived on 2026-11-22 from `specify-opening-funding-intent`, which was pending when this was written (qualification funding follows the exact funding order);
